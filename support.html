Support

Tell us what's wrong — it gets fixed fastest

GLPenFind is a small, independent project. One person reads every message, usually within two working days.

petroulakostofska@gmail.com

A price is wrong

Send the pharmacy name, the dose, the price you see on their site, and the link. If you can, include the date — prices change, and sometimes we're right about last Tuesday.

We re-read the page and correct it, or mark it as unread if the price has gone behind a gate.

A pharmacy is missing

Send the pharmacy's name and its website. It needs to publish a price publicly and be findable on the GPhC register — those are the only two requirements.

If it's regulated but publishes no price, we can still list it with the reason shown.

You're a pharmacy and want to be removed

Write from an address at the pharmacy's domain and we'll remove the listing. We don't argue about it, and we don't require a reason.

We won't remove a listing because a price is unflattering. The index only works if it isn't for sale.

A pharmacy appears unregistered

Tell us and we'll re-run the register check. Name matching on the register is loose, so genuine premises occasionally fail the tie-break and get dropped by mistake.

Please include the registration number if you have it — a number the pharmacy publishes itself is the strongest evidence there is.

What we can't help with

  • Orders, refunds, deliveries. We don't sell anything, so we can't see your order or chase it. Contact the pharmacy you bought from.
  • Medical questions. Whether a treatment is right for you, side effects, and dose changes are questions for a prescriber or your GP.
  • Account problems. There are no accounts. Nothing to reset, nothing to delete.

Privacy and data

The app collects nothing about you: no account, no analytics profile, no advertising identifier. The full policy is on the privacy page.
