Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026 · applies to the iOS app and the website

The short version: this app collects nothing about you. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no third-party scripts. The price list ships inside the app, so it works with no network connection at all.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not ask for your name, email, date of birth, weight, medication history or any other personal or health information, and there is no mechanism in the app that could send such information to us.

What is stored on your device

Only the bundled price list and your last selected dose. Neither leaves the device, and both are removed when you delete the app. The app sets no cookies.

Network connections

  • In the iOS app: none, unless you tap through to a pharmacy. Opening a pharmacy page hands you to your browser, and that pharmacy's own privacy policy applies from that point.
  • On the website: our hosting provider records standard request logs (IP address, browser type, page requested, time) for security and reliability. Those logs are not used to profile anyone and are not combined with any other data.
  • Diagnostics: the app contains no crash-reporting or analytics SDK. If you have opted in to sharing analytics with Apple, Apple may collect diagnostics under its own privacy policy; we do not receive a profile of you from it.

Pharmacies and links

We do not share anything with the pharmacies listed, and outbound links carry no referral or affiliate parameters. We are not told who clicks what. Prices and offers are read from each pharmacy's public pages; we are not a pharmacy, we do not sell medicine, and we take no payment for placement.

Health information

This app compares published prices and describes what each pharmacy asks for before it will prescribe. It does not give medical advice, does not assess whether any medicine is suitable for you, and does not store anything about your health. Decisions about treatment belong to you and a prescriber.

Children

The app is intended for adults. It is not directed at children and we knowingly collect no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to its processing. We hold none, so there is normally nothing to return or erase — but if you believe we hold something, write to the address below and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that collects anything — saving a pharmacy, syncing between devices, notifications — this page will say so before that version ships, the app will ask for your consent first, and the date above will change.
