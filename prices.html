Where the numbers come from

Privacy · About the app · Prices are per pen at the strength shown, as the pharmacy displays them, and change without warning — their checkout is the final word. Consultation and delivery charges are included only where the pharmacy states they are; delivery is shown on each entry where published. Every pharmacy listed is checked against the General Pharmaceutical Council register of pharmacy premises; the ones that cannot be verified are not shown. This is price and process information, not medical advice.

How this list is built

  • Registered only — every pharmacy is checked against the GPhC register of pharmacy premises. A pharmacy we cannot confirm is left out rather than listed with a caveat.
  • Price — read directly from the pharmacy's own page by our crawler, with the exact URL and timestamp stored. No aggregator's table is copied.
  • Consultation process — the checks the pharmacy itself says it will carry out: online questionnaire, photographs, video consultation, photo ID, GP or medical records, and any stated approval time. Listed as stated, not scored.
  • Rating — the pharmacy's own customer reviews, carried with the date they were read.

Where a pharmacy hides prices behind a consultation, we say so instead of guessing.
